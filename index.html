Obligation rate

Obligations and outlays as a share of the year's total budgetary resources
Why it matters for oversight: the exhibits on the other tabs say what was requested and enacted; USAspending says what the Department actually obligated and paid out. Pursestrings links those two records at the account level, so an under-execution signal in a given account can be traced back to the line items that were funded.
Extend this view: the same API exposes /federal_account/ and /program_activity/ for agency 097, and the DATA Act account files on USAspending give monthly obligations by Treasury account.

How the exhibits were normalized

  • Every file's header row was mapped to one schema: account, organization, budget activity, sub-activity, line (program element, budget line item, sub-activity group or military-personnel sub-activity), title, and amount by fiscal year.
  • For each fiscal year inside a file, one amount column was chosen: the "Total" column where one exists (base plus OCO, emergency, supplemental or reconciliation), otherwise the single column, otherwise base plus OCO summed. Quantity columns were ignored.
  • P-1 "Non-Add" rows (advance-procurement breakouts) were excluded so advance procurement is counted once; this is why P-1 sums are below the sheets' own "Total of Displayed Rows" check figure, which naively adds them. M-1, O-1 and R-1 sums match their check figures exactly.
  • Lines flagged "Include in TOA = N" (for example Defense Health Program RDT&E shown in the R-1) are kept and flagged, so the exhibits total as displayed.
  • The stage of a value comes from its position: the budget year is the request, the prior year is enacted, the year before that is actual. In four budgets the "enacted" column was in fact the request adjusted for a continuing resolution (PB2014 for FY2013, PB2018 for FY2017, PB2019 for FY2018, PB2025 for FY2024); those are labeled in the vintage view.
  • Lines are linked across years by exhibit, four-digit account, organization and line code; titles shown are from the most recent budget that carried the line. Older exhibits in all capitals were title-cased.
  • The FY2027 request columns are the exhibits' "Total" columns, which add the mandatory (reconciliation) request to the discretionary request; FY2026 totals likewise include the P.L. 119-21 spend plan. This is why FY2027 steps up sharply in every view.
  • Constant dollars use OMB's composite outlay deflator for national defense (Table 10.1), rebased to FY2027; the Green Book view uses the Comptroller's own FY2023 constant-dollar tables.
  • Not parsed: the FY2003 and FY2004 O-1 and the FY2003 to FY2005 M-1, which were published as formatted print layouts rather than tables; the FY2003 and FY2004 C-1, which had no appropriation column. Revolving funds (RF-1) and the reserve-component procurement view (P-1R, a subset of the P-1) are not included.
